Set Win Rocket [tennis]
Sharp 15-25¢ price spike immediately after winning first set, followed by sustained climb to 90¢+
▶ Buy within 2-3 minutes after set win confirmation when price spikes to 55-75¢ range ■ Exit when price reaches 90¢+ or shows first significant pullback of 5¢+ ⚠ Don't buy if opponent breaks serve immediately in next set
Cliff Drop Collapse [all]
Sudden 20-40¢ price crash from moderate levels to single digits when momentum shifts decisively
▶ Short when price drops below 40¢ after major score event (set loss, 10+ point deficit) ■ Cover when price reaches 5-10¢ or shows sustained bounce above 25¢ ⚠ Don't short if opponent shows any signs of struggle or inconsistency
Tiebreak Rollercoaster [tennis]
Extreme 15-30¢ price swings every few points during tiebreaks, creating untradeable volatility
▶ AVOID - Exit all positions before tiebreak begins ■ Wait for tiebreak resolution before re-entering ⚠ Any attempt to trade individual tiebreak points
Late Match Rocket [all]
Explosive 20-40¢ price surge in final 10-15% of match as outcome becomes certain
▶ Buy when price breaks above 70¢ with clear momentum in final 20% of match ■ Hold until 95¢+ or match completion - momentum rarely reverses ⚠ Don't enter if opponent has shown any late-game resilience
False Dawn Collapse [all]
Price spikes 10-20¢ on temporary momentum then crashes below starting point
▶ Short when price spikes above 60¢ without sustained score improvement ■ Cover when price drops below 30¢ or shows genuine momentum reversal ⚠ Don't short if spike is backed by multiple consecutive score events
Heavy Favorite Steamroll [all]
Steady climb from 75¢+ to 99¢ with minimal volatility as favorite dominates
▶ Buy heavy favorites (75¢+) on any dip below 85¢ after establishing control ■ Hold until 95¢+ or first sign of opponent resistance ⚠ Don't buy if opponent shows early fight or closes score gap
Death Spiral [all]
Relentless price decline from moderate levels to near-zero with brief false hope bounces
▶ Short any bounce above 20¢ when clear losing momentum established ■ Cover when price reaches single digits or match nears completion ⚠ Don't short if opponent shows inconsistency or fatigue
Break Point Volatility [tennis]
Rapid 5-10¢ swings during crucial service games and break points
▶ Buy dips during deuce situations when favorite is serving ■ Exit immediately after game completion - hold or break confirmed ⚠ Don't hold positions through multiple deuce points
Halftime Rocket [ncaab_men]
Sharp 10-20¢ price movement after halftime based on first half performance
▶ Buy immediately at halftime if first half showed clear dominance ■ Exit after 5-8% of second half or at first major score reversal ⚠ Don't buy if halftime lead was built on opponent mistakes rather than own skill
Dead Cat Bounce [all]
Brief 5-10¢ price recovery during losing effort before final collapse
▶ Short any bounce above 20¢ when underlying momentum clearly negative ■ Cover when bounce fails and price resumes decline ⚠ Don't short if bounce coincides with opponent fatigue or errors
